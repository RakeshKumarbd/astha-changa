Astha Changa — Help

How do I play?

In the game, tap How to play, or ? during a game. It lists the exact rules of the rule set you're using.

What do the cowries mean?

With the standard rules, count the shells that land open (mouth up). 1, 2 or 3 open move that many squares. All 4 closed is a 4 (Changa) and all 4 open is an 8 (Ashta). A 4 or 8 gives you another throw.

Why can't my piece go inside?

With the standard rules, you must cut at least one opponent piece before your pieces can leave the outer ring. You can switch this off by making your own rule set: Rules → New.

Can I play with my family's rules?

Yes. Tap New or Edit next to Rules to change the board size, number of shells, the moves, safe squares and more. Share sends your rules to others, who add them with Import rules.

How many people can play?

2–6 on the 5×5 board and up to 8 on the bigger boards, as people or computer players. With 4, 6 or 8 players you can play in two teams.

Is my game saved?

Yes. The game saves after every move on your device. Tap Resume game on the main screen.

कैसे खेलें?

कौड़ियों का मतलब?

खुली (मुँह ऊपर) कौड़ियाँ गिनें: 1, 2 या 3 खुली = उतने घर। चारों बंद = 4 (चंगा), चारों खुली = 8 (अष्टा)। 4 या 8 पर एक और दाँव।

अपने घर के नियम?

नियम के पास नया या बदलें दबाकर अपने नियम बनाएँ, और शेयर करें से दूसरों को भेजें।

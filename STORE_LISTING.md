## Description (both stores)

```
Astha Changa — also called Ashta Chamma, Chowka Bara or Indian Ludo — is the race game that families in India have played for generations on a board drawn on paper, with four cowrie shells instead of dice.

THROW THE COWRIES
Count the shells that land open. All four closed is a Changa (4), all four open is an Ashta (8), and both give you another throw. Spend each throw on any piece you like.

CUT, RACE AND REACH THE CENTRE
Move round the outer ring, cut your opponents' pieces to send them home, then spiral inward to the centre. The crossed squares are safe. First to bring every piece home wins.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 2 to 8 players: people, computer players (easy, medium, hard), or a mix
• Play in two teams with 4, 6 or 8 players
• Pass-the-phone mode for playing together on one device
• 5×5, 7×7 and 9×9 boards
• Make your own rules: number of shells, moves, safe squares, direction, cutting rules and more
• Share your family's rules with a code
• English and Hindi

MADE TO FEEL LIKE THE REAL THING
A paper board, crossed safe squares, tumbling cowries, and seeds and beans for pieces, each with its own shape.

NO ADS. NO ACCOUNTS. NO DATA COLLECTED.
Works fully offline. Your games are saved on your device, and you can carry on any time.
```
